# T14 — Panels as a ROM table: word_3007924 (type→flag word) and PanelOffsetListsPointerTable

## Result
M1's panels line went from `DERIVED-FROM-CODE: asm/object.s routines; no type->routine table
located | 0 / 13` to `FOUND: word_3007924 (IWRAM copy, asm/asm38.s:4242-4249) =
IWRAMRoutinesROMLocation+0x1E24 = 0x081D7E24 in ROM (bn6f.map:34342; copied by start.s:57-63 to
0x3005B00 len 0x1ed4): 13 words, stride 4, one per panel type 0x0..0xC, OR-ed into
oPanelData_Flags by _object_updatePanelParameters (asm/asm38.s:4213-4219) | 8 / 13`.

The ROM original: the three searches the ticket predicted would fail did fail for a *named data
label* — (1) `grep "0x18000" asm/ data/` hits only asm38.s:4243 (the IWRAM listing), (2)
`grep "0x10050"` hits only asm38.s:4244, (3) `grep "3007924|1D7E24"` hits asm38.s, bn6f.elf
(binary) and bn6f.map. What worked instead: a byte search of /tmp/bn6f_real.gba for the 13-word
pattern (00 80 01 00 | 00 40 01 00 | 10 00 01 00 | 50 00 01 00 | …) found exactly one match at
ROM offset 0x001D7E24 → 0x081D7E24. bn6f.map:34342 gives `IWRAMRoutinesROMLocation = 0x081d6000`,
and start.s:57-63 copies 0x1ed4 bytes from it to 0x3005B00 — 0x3005B00 + 0x1E24 = 0x3007924. So
the table's ROM original is IWRAMRoutinesROMLocation+0x1E24; it has no label of its own in data/
because it travels inside the IWRAM-code blob.
