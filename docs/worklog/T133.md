## Step 2 (loader order / the +1 vs +512 offsets) — MEASURED

- **Tiles-then-map.** `sub_8080DA0` (asm/asm21.s:14-45) per battle-BG: `SetBGScrollCallbacks`
  → LCD control → `LoadBGAnimData` (:33) → `LoadGFXAnims` (:37, the animated art schedule
  `off_807FB98`), called from `initBattleStructsAndVram_80071D4` (asm/asm00_1.s:8435).
  Inside `LoadBGAnimData` (asm/asm03_0.s:21211-21268): palette queue, then GFX
  decompress→`CopyByEightWords` to `GFXDest` (:21223-21242), then tilemap
  decompress→copy to `TilemapDestOffset + iBGTileIdBlocks_Ptr` (:21244-21266). So within one
  BG the tiles land before the map, and the BG's static load precedes the anim schedule.
- **Canon's +1.** The anim script's initial copy is `gfx_anim_4bit_tile_copy
  gfx_dest=unk_6000040 num_tiles=0x24` (dat20.s:149) — tile indices 2..37 of charbase 0;
  slot 1 (0x06000020) is the blank filler; the map (screenbase 29 = 0x0600E800, F47) names
  cells MAP+1 (T22's measured relation). I did not find the single instruction that bakes the
  +1 (the tilemap reaches VRAM through a compressed blob at `BGAnimData.TilemapSrc` copied by
  LoadBGAnimData :21244-21266 — the +1 is plausibly IN that blob, not added at runtime);
  flagged unverified, the measured relation stands either way.
- **Port's +512.** agb's tile allocator (vendor/agb/agb/src/display/tiled/vram_manager/
  tile_allocator.rs:26-40) reserves tile 0 for the blank tile and starts the regular 4bpp
  pool at `AFFINE_ALLOC_END` (VRAM+64, tile index 2); each distinct tile index of a TileSet
  is allocated at its first `set_tile` (regular_background.rs:140-199). The backdrop's
  tileset therefore starts at the first free index after everything allocated before it —
  measured 512 (0x06004000) on F47's kept dumps, and the port's cell ids are base+MAP.

## Conclusions

- **The "port not slotwise faithful, 1/37" caveat is dead by measurement, not by a code
  change.** The port's resident window is 37/37 under the composition-correct convention:
  slot 512+j holds `FRAMES[step][firstocc[j]]`, firstocc = the asset MAP's row-major
  first-occurrence order -- confirmed at every sampled capture on the kept dumps, step
  sequence matching canon's. The permutation's provenance is therefore no longer a
  hypothesis: it IS the map-scan order (agb allocates at first `set_tile`), and the +512
  cell-id offset is agb's first-free-tile base. Canon's side is 37/37 as `slot k =
  FRAMES[step][k-1]` (T22, reproduced). Making the port's ORDER literally canon's (FRAMES
  order at consecutive indices) requires controlling agb's allocator; both attempts above
  broke the frozen table, so the caveat is resolved as EXPLAINED rather than ELIMINATED.
- **field-bg1's (and bg2's) residue is one frame, k=0**, the F45/T31 self.shown layer-
  renumber transition edge: at rust capture 121 our BG0 sliver is still the pre-renumber
  layer (solid black), the backdrop lands on BG0 at capture 122 and matches canon from
  there. Owner: src/battle.rs:4899-4910 (NOT list) and/or the frozen align -- not fixable
  inside this ticket. Neither the tile permutation nor the timer/seed owns any of the
  residue (no timer band exists: 39 of 40 frames read 0).
- **The review-vs-today drift (3120 -> 5280 bg1, 0 -> 5280 bg2, 380131 -> 380263 bg3,

- The 0xff map-default art's other steps and whether field-bg3's 380263 shares the k=0

  `LoadBGAnimData` (asm/asm03_0.s:21209) / `sub_8080DA0` (asm/asm21.s:15-45) — NOT YET READ.
