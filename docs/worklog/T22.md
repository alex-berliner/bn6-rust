Layout facts this rests on (from step 1 + F47's kept map dumps): canon resident slots are 1..37 in
the 0x06000000:0x800 window (slot k at window offset k*32), and canon slot k holds asset tile
`FRAMES[step][k-1]` — i.e. the tables index slots 2..37 and slot 1 is the constant blank. Rust
resident slots are 512..548 (map ids 0/512..548 at 0x06009800), i.e. window offsets 0..1152.

## Step 3 — the naive `s*37+k` control — reproduces F47's 2-3/37

**[SUPERSEDED by the verifier audit, see the audit section at the end: the exact digits below
were convention-muddled — the audit's pinned figures are best 2/37 (cap 174, cap 39), no step
at 3, +1-shifted variant 36/37. The off-by-one conclusion stands; the exact-3/37 claim is

tile for canon slot k is `s*37+k` — off by ONE against the truth `s*37+(k-1)` (slot k ↔ row index

- off-by-one-tile convention (slot k vs `ts[s*37+k-1]`): `[2,3,2,2,1,2,2]` per step — **step 1 →
  3/37 = F47's `{1:3}` exactly**;
- 1-based-slot convention (slot k vs `ts[s*37+k]`): `[3,3,2,1,2,1,2]` — best 3/37;
- (true layout: 37/37, step 2 resident at cap 174).

So the naive grid reads 2-3/37 where the truth is 37/37 — F47's number was a pure off-by-one
between 1-based VRAM slot ids and 0-based FRAMES row indices, on top of which the *rust* side's
map-scan permutation would have broken any slot-ordered test anyway. Both failure modes named.
