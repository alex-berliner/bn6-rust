Layout facts this rests on (from step 1 + F47's kept map dumps): canon resident slots are 1..37 in
the 0x06000000:0x800 window (slot k at window offset k*32), and canon slot k holds asset tile
`FRAMES[step][k-1]` — i.e. the tables index slots 2..37 and slot 1 is the constant blank. Rust
resident slots are 512..548 (map ids 0/512..548 at 0x06009800), i.e. window offsets 0..1152.
