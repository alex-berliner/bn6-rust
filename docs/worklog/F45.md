   capture can show. `field-bg2` reading byte-identical on 39 of 40 frames is the strongest
   single piece of evidence (a wrong pairing compares panels against backdrop and saturates).

   backdrop BY ELIMINATION: panels 0 on 39/40 frames, HUD 0 until canon's own RESULT
   slide-in starts at canon 154
   (= post k=19, exact 1917 px/frame ramp saturating at 24906 = the RESULT window area,
   matching the `warp` row's measured slide rate) — that part is the canon RESULT-over-HUD
   vs rust HUD-on-BG2/shown-on-BG3 stack difference, invisible in the integrated composite
   (no integ spike at k>=19), not a HUD defect.
3. By-index bookkeeping closes trivially: 14204 (k=2..4) + 5606 (k=5,
   boundary transition frame) +
   139125 (k=6..39) = 158935 — a partition of the field row's own integrated per-frame
   sums, NOT a sum of the mask totals (367776/4560/380263 are never summed against it).
   Layers are assigned by elimination only; windows and blend/mosaic unexcluded.
   Rejected frames: 0. Uncovered: 0.
4. Windows: `--disable-bg` and `--only-bg N` both force disableWIN[0]/[1]=true
   (tools/mgba_capture.c:396-398, :549-550) — re-read and confirmed this session. A window
   claim cannot come from any isolation mechanism. BLEND/BLDCNT/BLDALPHA/BLDY/mosaic: grep
   across tools/ empty, no flag touches them, untested — could in principle carry
   composite-only residue; noted in the coverage doc with why. With every other BG
   disabled there is nothing for blend to blend with, so a blend-only or window-only
   composite difference is structurally invisible to all three masks — hence unexcluded.
