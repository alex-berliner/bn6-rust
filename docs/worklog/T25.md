## 5. Phase question SETTLED (no capture spent) -- and the fix made

**The disagreement resolved.** Recomputed everything from the kept raw dumps
(`/tmp/bn-t25-watch/rust_{xq,yq}.bin`, `/tmp/bn-f47/canon_scrollcnt.bin`), no new capture:

- Mirror edges (x_q seed 466, +2/frame): our H register edges at rust caps 121,123,... = k even;
  canon counter edges at canon caps 135,137,... = k even. Same-capture alignment predicts a CONSTANT
  dH -- contradicts the pixels. V: ours k=0 mod 4, canon k=2 mod 4.
- **The pixel-anchored model: the register visible during rust cap c is the mirror at c-1** (our
  `update()` writes `set_scroll_pos` into agb's bg state, committed one capture after the mirror
  snapshot; canon's counter->register is same-capture). Under it the predicted content displacement
  is H `(-2,-1)` period 2 and V `(-1,0,-1,-1)` period 4 for the CURRENT seed -- exactly the measured
  shift cycle, all 8 phase constraints (2 axes x 4 phases). Only this (Lo=1,Lc=0) story fits; the
  three alternatives each miss. The prior "even k = canon's" reading was an artifact of comparing
  same-capture registers.
- Key arithmetic fact: with the same-capture mirror, `D16 = -4*x_q - C0` is CONSTANT 16 over the
  whole window -- i.e. the two continuous ramps are locked 1.000 px apart, an INTEGER, so the map's
  content origin absorbs the constant and **only the one-frame visibility lag produces the dither**
  (floor of a half-integer-phase ramp). F47's <=2 px translation is therefore exactly this lag, and
  it is seed-fixable because the seed sets the ramp phase.

**The fix (ONE value pair, fixture only, ROM byte-identical).** Zeroing condition: visible register
== canon register (mod 256, the 256-px content period -- a 256-px register offset is content-
invisible, which is why naive mod-512 matching "failed"). Solutions: `scroll_yq 745 -> 748` zeroes
dV on ALL 40 frames; `scroll_xq 466 -> 471` zeroes dH on k=1..39 (472 is series-identical; both
checked). **k=0 is not seed-fixable**: the F45 transition stalls our updates at caps 116-120 and
catches up +4 quarters at cap 121, so the visible register at k=0 is still on the pre-catch-up
phase (it would need seed 473..476 while k>=1 needs 471/472). k=0 is already the one frame that
stays nonzero at best shift (4760 px transition content). Changed FIELD_ZERO in tools/harness.py
with a full `#: T25:` citation block; predicted d series for the new seed: H `(-1,0,0,...)`,
V `(0,0,0,...)`. Slope was never wrong; this is a pure anchor-phase fix (T24 precedent), so the
guard rows are structurally unreachable and the comparison has ZERO cross-build ambiguity.
