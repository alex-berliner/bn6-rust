## Step 3 — What off_80F24D8 / off_80F253C / off_80F25A0 really are

The three labels in the section header are 25-entry parallel tables:
- `off_80F2474` -- unnamed, 25 entries, **2 callsites**:
  - the per-ai pattern routine for sub_80F2354 (bx r0, asm31.s:123336) — 22
    nullsub_106 + sub_80F651C (slot 5) + sub_80FAE5C (slot 10) + sub_81003A8
    (slot 16) — only 3 of 25 have a real per-ai routine
- `off_80F23AC` -- unnamed, 25 entries, 1 callsite:
  - passed as the jump-table arg to ai_eventuallyRunsAIAttack_801AF44
    (asm31.s:123344-123346)
- `off_80F2410` -- unnamed, 25 entries, 1 callsite:
  - the FIRST routine called by sub_80F2354 (bx r0, asm31.s:123330); shared
    damage routines (sub_801A9B8/sub_801AA48/sub_801AAC0/sub_801AB40 at
    asm00_2.s:22868/22923/22964/23009) — only 4 distinct symbols across 25
    slots, NOT per-Navi
- `NaviActHandlers_80F25A0` -- 25 entries, 1 callsite:
  - the LAST routine called by sub_80F2354 (bx r0, asm31.s:123349) — 22
    nullsub_106 + sub_80F2A1E (slot 0) + sub_80F650A (slot 5) +
    sub_80FDEFC (slot 13)
- `NaviEnemyStruct1Ptrs_80F24D8` (the table the inventory currently calls
  "struct1") -- 25 entries, NOT consumed by sub_80F2354 (consumed by
  enemy_getStruct1, asm/asm00_2.s:669-674, the virus access path)
- `NaviEnemyStruct2Ptrs_80F253C` (the table the inventory currently calls
  "struct2") -- 25 entries, NOT consumed by sub_80F2354 (consumed by
  enemy_getStruct2, asm/asm00_2.s:713, the virus access path)

**Why 22 of 25 `act` slots are `nullsub_106` in the current parse**: the
inventory reads `NaviActHandlers_80F25A0` -- 22 of 25 entries there really are
nullsub_106 (the canonical text). The same 22 are nullsub_106 in the
sibling `off_80F2474` table. The pattern work these navis do is split into:
- the CurAction-indexed handler table reached via
  `AIThinkTables_8109050[ai_index]` (the THINK arm in the virus chain — this
  is what asm31.s:169395-169402 calls "the per-AIIndex state machine
  pointer"), and
- the rare per-ai routines reached via `off_80F2474[ai]` and
  `NaviActHandlers_80F25A0[ai]` (only slots 0, 5, 10, 13, 16 have non-null
  entries).

**Reader line**: sub_80F2354 (asm/asm31.s:123323-123356) is the reader; it is
reached by `naviObject_dispatch_80F2330` (asm/asm31.s:123304-123312) at
CurState == 1 (r5 = oBattleObject_AIDataPtr, reads oAIData_AIIndex, lsls by 2,
then loads off_80F239C/off_80F23A4/off_80F23A0/off_80F23A8 = pointers to the
4 parallel 25-entry tables).

## What this ticket actually changes

NEGATIVE-shaped result: the 25-row "navis + cybeasts" inventory is reading
the WRONG three tables. The real per-Navi pattern data is in the same
`AIThinkTables_8109050` chain the viruses use, plus two extra 25-entry tables
(`off_80F2474` and `NaviActHandlers_80F25A0`). Of the 25 indexes:
- 22 share a `nullsub_106` "act" cell at `NaviActHandlers_80F25A0` AND a
  nullsub_106 cell at `off_80F2474` — both can be reported as a single
  "no per-ai routine; pattern work is in the CurAction-handler table at
  `AIThinkTables_8109050[ai]`"
- 3 (slots 0, 5, 13) have a named NaviActHandlers routine
- 3 (slots 5, 10, 16) have a named off_80F2474 routine
- the real "what pattern does this Navi run" answer is the per-AIIndex
  CurAction table — for AI 0x01 that is `ForMettaur_8109EF4`
  (asm/asm31.s:169450 -> 171012-170972).

The parse_navis rewrite has been done as the smallest change that surfaces
both the per-Navi arm and the per-ai routine for the few navis that have one,
plus the existing NAVI_ enum naming for slots 0..11 (slots 12..24 remain GAP
rows because no name string lives in the disassembly — the 13 unnamed
indexes the ticket mentions are real GAPS at this level; sprite categories
