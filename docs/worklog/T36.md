**The only ID-keyed lookup in the Cannon arm is the palette:**
`barrel.set_palette_add((chip.id - CHIP_CANNON) as usize)` at
src/battle.rs:4206. Since chip.subfamily is 0/1/2 for Cannon/HiCannon/
M-Cannon (matching chip.id - 1 because no other chip uses family 0x14
subs 0..2 in our 48-record asset), the palette reads the same row in
byte_80EBD2C either way (asm31.s:109657, `byte 0x0, 0x1, 0x2, 0x1E, ...
` — first three entries are the per-subfamily palette for the cannon
barrel, read at asm31.s:109551 `ldrb r0, [r1, r0]` with r0 = the
subfamily stored in oAIAttackVars_Unk_03 = the record's
attack_subfamily). One-for-one agreement; the family-0x14 dispatch
replaces the chip.id indexing.
