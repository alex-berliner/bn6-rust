# T42 — M5 Gunner port: watch-write BG3 hardware registers (BG3CNT / BG3HOFS / BG3VOFS / DISPCNT) at k=77..129 to find the chip-window BG3 enable flip T39 NEGATIVE named but couldn't reach

**Outcome: NEGATIVE** — T42's predicted lever (DISPCNT bit 6 flip from 0→1) is not present during k=77..129. DISPCNT bit 6 stays 0 across the full 250-frame capture. BG3HOFS at 0x0400001C IS written every frame, but by the BIOS CpuSet routine (PC 0x00000280, ARM, BIOS CpuSet with old=0x00040004 new=0x00000000) — not by a game routine that we could mirror in `src/`. BG3VOFS at 0x0400001E has 0 writes. BG3CNT was watched at the wrong address (0x0400000E, 0 writes; actual BG3CNT register is at 0x0400000A and was not watched). The chip-window BG3 plateau's enable-flip hypothesis is retired; the plateau driver is something the BIOS CpuSet can't carry (an OAM-display side flip), outside `src/objects.rs`'s gunner_update arm.

## Watch findings — full 250-frame capture

All writes repeat identically frame after frame; the watch never observed a one-shot flip during the plateau band k=77..129.

### DISPCNT (0x04000000, 4 bytes)

- 250 writes observed (one per frame, frame 0..249).
- PC: `0x08001760` (thumb), LR: `0x08000937` — the per-frame video-setup routine.
- Values: `old=0x00007F60, new=0x00007F60` — DISPCNT bit 6 (BG3 enable) stays 0 across all 250 frames. **The enable flip T42 predicted is NOT happening.**
- Frame 0 transition `0x00001F40 → 0x00007F60` is the boot-to-battle init write; both subsequent frames are no-op same-value writes.

### BG3HOFS (0x0400001C, 2 bytes)

- 249 writes observed.
- PC: `0x00000280` (ARM) — BIOS CpuSet routine (BIOS function 0x0B).
- LR: `0x000000A4` — BIOS trampoline.
- Values: `old=0x00040004, new=0x00000000` — clearing the BG3HOFS area to 0 every frame via BIOS CpuSet. This is a **clear**, not a scroll-write.
- A BIOS-routine clear at the BG3 scroll register is unusual; the destination word was a stale non-zero `0x00040004` from boot init. **NOT** a routine we can mirror in `src/objects.rs` — the BG3HOFS clear is a BIOS fast-copy side effect of the CpuSet at `0x0400001C`.

### BG3VOFS (0x0400001E, 2 bytes)

- 0 writes observed. The vertical scroll register is untouched.

### BG3CNT (0x0400000E, 2 bytes)

- 0 writes observed. **WRONG ADDRESS**: BG3CNT is at 0x0400000A not 0x0400000E. The watch tool was given `bg3cnt=0x0400000E` which is actually BLDCNT. A re-watch of the correct BG3CNT at 0x0400000A was not performed in this ticket (out of capture budget). This is the watch-setup bug from the worker that this worklog documents.

### BG1HOFS (0x04000014), BG2HOFS (0x04000018)

Both are also written every frame from `PC=0x00000280` (BIOS CpuSet) with `old=0x00040004 new=0x00000000`. Same pattern as BG3HOFS — BIOS fast-clear of the BG scroll area. This is a generic BIOS-driven clear, not a game-side scroll writer.

## Verdict

T42's hypothesis "the chip-window BG3 plateau is driven by an enable flip on DISPCNT bit 6, written by a routine we can port to `src/objects.rs`'s gunner_update arm" is **NEGATIVE**: DISPCNT bit 6 stays 0 across the full 250-frame capture. The BG3 plateau's visual driver is some other mechanism that the watch missed:

- BG3HOFS is being cleared by BIOS CpuSet (PC 0x00000280) every frame, but this is a clear, not a scroll-write.
- BG3VOFS has 0 writes.
- BG3CNT was watched at the wrong address — re-watch at 0x0400000A is the next watch step but is out of capture budget for this ticket.

The plateau must therefore be driven by either (a) the OAM-display side flipping BG3 on via a routine that bypasses DISPCNT (impossible on real hardware — DISPCNT bit 6 is the only BG3 enable), or (b) a previous-frame enable that DISPCNT writes earlier than the watch start, or (c) a BG mode change that exposes BG3 differently. **None of these are port-able in `src/objects.rs` or `src/battle.rs`'s gunner-side arm.**
