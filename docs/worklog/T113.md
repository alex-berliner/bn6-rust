- **No ai_index-keyed arm table exists in `src/ai.rs`.** Dispatch is the
  hand-written `Style` enum (`Thrust`/`Divide`/`Gunner`/`Mettaur`); the only
  per-type port is `Style::Mettaur` -> `MettaurEntry` =
  `ForMettaur_8109EF4` (asm31.s:170982), i.e. **ai_index 1's think entry**.
- **ai_index 4 is ABSENT as a think arm, and the SPAWN is silently shared
  with ai 1.** `src/battle.rs:2148` and `:2165` construct
  `ai::Ai::new(ai::Style::Mettaur)` unconditionally for every fixture enemy,
  and the sprite is hardwired `spr::Assets::new(METTAUR)` at
  `src/battle.rs:2142`/`:2159`. `fixture.rs`'s per-slot kind bytes
  (`enemy_kind`/`kind_of`, KIND_METTAUR=0/KIND_GUNNER=1, fixture.rs:348-356)
  are read by NOTHING in `src/` — even the Gunner row's rust side fields two
  Mettaur-sprite Mettaur-AI enemies. So an ai-4 virus fielded through
  today's descriptor is silently fielded AS a Mettaur.
- **ROM side (the cite the port will need).** `AIThinkTables_8109050`
  (asm/asm31.s:169448, indexed by ai_index*4 — comment at asm31.s:171011):
  entry 0x10 = **`off_810B2D0`** (label asm31.s:173621; the ticket's

  `sub_8109804`, `sub_810B042`, `sub_810B06A`,
  `genericAI_movementRelated_8109952`, `sub_810B172`
  (asm31.s:173622-173637).
- **Identity/Struct2 bytes verified by direct ROM read** (this ticket,
