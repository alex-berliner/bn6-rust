# F39a — opening integrated 25829/931/40: the four OBJ entries canon does not draw, and the per-sprite PAL_OBJ slot

Worked on branch `wt/f39a-obj-pal` (off 0c44aaa). **Outcome: no src/ change.** The ticket's
premise — that the four rust-only OAM entries are enemy-object sprite entries suppressed by a
canon VISIBLE/Unk_03 gate — is refuted by measurement; the pairs are the enemy HP-readout
digits, gated in canon by intro completion, and the fix belongs to a battle.rs readout-gate
ticket the coordinator declined to open mid-flight.

## Step 1 — settle the value (capture run 1: `verify_rows.py HEAD opening`; capture run 2: `harness.py --only opening --ui integrated --no-gallery`)

- `python3 tools/verify_rows.py HEAD opening --expect 'opening:integrated=F'` at HEAD **0c44aaa**
  prints `opening PASS 0/0/40/86591`.
- **Parser trap (the root of F38i's false conflict):** `verify_rows.py`'s `parse()` keys the
  result dict by row name only (`out.setdefault(m["row"], ...)`) and the harness prints the
  **isolated** line first, so verify_rows reports the ISOLATED numbers for any `ui="both"` row.
  The `--expect opening:integrated=F` form is silently ignored (the key never matches, verdict
  stays "ran"). So F38i's "already 0/0 on main HEAD [8b314eb]" was the isolated line, not a
  contradiction of the scoreboard.
- `tools/harness.py --only opening --ui integrated --no-gallery` on the same clean HEAD 0c44aaa:
  `opening integrated FAILED total 25829 worst 931 frames 40 | negative: not blind (total 105749)`.
- **HEAD's truth: opening integrated = 25829/931/40** (matches the 1f81767 scoreboard);
  isolated 0/0/40. Fitted constants at HEAD: **19** (derived 429, peeked 145).

## Step 3 — the emitter, measured

The pairs are the **enemy HP readouts**, not enemy sprite parts:

- `src/hud.rs:133-135` + `:16-17`: digits are drawn as "one 8x16 object per digit", GLYPH_W=8,
  GLYPH_BYTES=0x40; METTAUR_HP=40 (`src/battle.rs:47`) → two digits '4','0' = exactly two 8x16
  entries 8 px apart.
- `draw_number_in` right-aligns at `px + self.hud.width(hp)/2`; enemy0 panel (5,1) →
  `panel_centre` (180,84) (`src/field.rs:395-396`), width("40")=16 → right edge 188 → digits at
  x=180 and x=172, y=84 — **exact match with pair 1**. Pair 2 = enemy1's readout at (5,3)=(180,132).
- The digit sprites are the HUD's first-uploaded DynamicSprite16s → VRAM tiles 0..15 (attr2 tile
  0 and 8), and they use the HUD palette slot → OAM pal 2. All three observed fields explained.
- Timing: pair 1 is up from k=0 (enemy0 targetable before the window), pair 2 appears at k=30 —
  the frame enemy1's `Action::Appearing` (APPEAR_FRAMES=32) ends and `is_targetable()`
  (`src/actor.rs:538-545`) flips true. The emitting path is the per-enemy readout loop in
  `src/battle.rs` (~4837-4862): `self.enemies.iter().zip(self.hp_shown.iter().skip(1))
  .filter(|(a,_)| a.is_present() && a.hp() > 0 && a.is_targetable())` → `hud.draw_number_in`.
- Canon side: sub_801641A (asm00_2.s:16101-16136) **sets** OBJECT_FLAG_VISIBLE (0x02) during the
  materialise ticks (loc_801644C branch) and writes phase 8 at materialise end (:16150-16156);
  canon's materialising enemies are visible and do emit their 3-part clusters (canon k=31/32
  12→15 confirms). Canon simply draws **no HP digits through the whole intro** — its readout
  first appears after all three enemies have materialised (past k=39 in this row). So a
  VISIBLE/Unk_03 emission gate on the enemy object cannot remove the pairs: they are HUD digit
  objects emitted by battle.rs, not the enemy object's OAM entries, and canon's own gate is the
  intro/battle-state completion, not the object flag.
- `object_spawnType1` (asm00_1.s:299-316) only allocates + sub_8003400 — hides nothing; nothing
  in it suppresses an entry either.

## What was NOT done, and why

- **No VISIBLE gate shipped.** The coordinator's mid-run steering directed gating sprite emission
  on `flags & 0x02`; the supervisor then ruled (after seeing the step 2/3 attribution) that the
  gate is measured-as-inapplicable — it would have moved nothing — and that a battle-state gate
  on the readout in src/battle.rs is outside this ticket's named Files and must not be widened to.
- **No PAL_OBJ port.** Step 4's `sub_8002818` port (sprite.s:254-303, slot = `Unk_15 >> 4` into
  0x03001550+slot*32, a pinned field vs our first-fit `try_allocate_shared`, src/spr.rs:699-763)
  was not started: the supervisor froze src/ changes after the attribution refuted the premise.
  The slot-shift fingerprint (rust 0-5 vs canon 0-15; pal[0]=canon[14], pal[2]=canon[0],
  pal[4]=canon[12]) is unchanged at HEAD 0c44aaa.
- **F38i's stub:** `pal_obj_allocate` is **not on this tree** (grep over src/: absent; it stayed
  on F38i's unmerged branch). Nothing to remove; no dead code committed here.
