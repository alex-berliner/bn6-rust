NOT a single attack_family. Reading `data/ChipDataArr.s` (411 records, index =

## Step 2: refactor (LANDED — commit 78cab3d)

After two intermediate commits (`d646a91`, `312963f`) that widened the change to
`bomb_anim` (element-driven) and `seed_or_bomb_palette`/`bomb_palette` (also
element/family-driven), the element-driven `bomb_anim` regressed chip-energbom
and chip-megenbom to FAILED 6895/146/60/22195 — `bomb_anim(0x06, false)`
returned `BOMB_ANIM_ENER_HELD (2)` and `bomb_anim(0x06, true)` returned
`BOMB_ANIM_ENER_THROWN (3)`, byte-for-byte equivalent to the old id-driven
match arm, but the resulting pixel output differed. Reverted `bomb_anim` to
`u16`/id-driven.

Reverted `seed_or_bomb_palette` and `bomb_palette` to id-driven too:
BlkBomb (family 0x12 sub 0x01, NONE element) and BigBomb (family 0x12 sub 0x0F,
NONE element) need palettes 4 and 3 respectively (data/ChipDataArr.s:1713 +
byte_80C5BA0[3]:29422), and the only way to recover those from the record is
to dispatch on family+subfamily — but `seed_or_bomb_palette` only has the
element in scope, so its call sites would have to grow to forward family and
subfamily too, widening the change to the seed/BugBomb/VDoll arms that are
OUT OF SCOPE. Kept id-driven to preserve BlkBomb's held palette 4.

The in-scope data-driven change that survived:

- `wide: chip.id == CHIP_BIGBOMB` →
  `wide: chip.family == BOMB_FAMILY_BLK_BIG && chip.subfamily == BOMB_SUB_BIGBOMB`
  (0x12/0x0F, data/ChipDataArr.s:6265; no other chip uses that pair in the asset).

The new constants added but currently unused (kept for the follow-up that
will extend the bomb path without re-deriving them):
`CHIP_ELEM_CURSOR_U8`, `CHIP_ELEM_NONE_U8`, `BOMB_FAMILY_X_MINI`,
`BOMB_FAMILY_ENER_FLASH`, `BOMB_FAMILY_BLK_BIG`, `BOMB_SUB_BLKBOMB`,
`BOMB_SUB_BIGBOMB`, `BOMB_SUB_ENER_FLASH`, `BOMB_SUB_MINI`.

## Step 4: inventory.py (not landed)

Step 5 of the ticket asks for the bomb family rows in `tools/inventory.py` to
flip from `verified-pixels` to `verified`. The current status logic keys on
`attack_family in AS_DATA_FAMILIES` (currently `{0x13}` for sword/blade only).
The bomb family spans THREE attack_families (0x2F, 0x29, 0x12), and 0x12 is
also shared by LilBolr1/2/3, the three seeds, BugBomb, and VDoll — adding 0x12
to `AS_DATA_FAMILIES` would widen the "verified" status to chips OUT OF SCOPE
for this ticket (the seeds especially, per T34 NEGATIVE). A targeted mechanism
(e.g. an `AS_DATA_SUBFAMILIES` set covering just the four bomb rows) is the
right shape but is its own refactor and was not attempted in this commit to
keep the diff minimal — the user-facing acceptance (0/0/N on all six rows) is
already met by the bomb_anim revert.
