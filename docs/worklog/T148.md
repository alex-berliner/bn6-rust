# T148 — M3 Counter Hit → Full Synchro — RECON-ONLY, premise not confirmed (no port)

**Status.** Blocked on a premise correction. The ticket's specified mechanism ("grant = write 1 to
0x0203528F, emotion byte") is contradicted by every measurement taken here. Per the ticket rule
("if the number did not move as the ticket predicted, say so and stop"), **no src/ change was made**.
Everything below is measurement, done on the mettaur scenario
(`bn6f_sterile.gba`, `pausedwithcannon.state`, ALIVE cheats `0x0203ab84:0xffff`+`0x0203ab86:0xffff`,
`--script Start@10,A@F`) with probe.py watch / mgba_capture --watch-write.

## 1. 0x0203528F is NOT the counter/grant byte (re-measured)

`eStruct2035280+0xf` = 0x0203528F stayed **0x00 through every run** (in-window press A@40, out-of-window
A@60, second-cycle A@140; 100+ frames each, --every 1). This confirms docs/coverage/emotion.md (T122):
+0x0f is the 12-step blink countdown owned by sub_801CC94; it only ever takes 0xc at blink-arm. There is
no damage-path writer of 0x0203528F on this route: a `--watch-write 0x02035288:4` capture (130 frames,
A@40) shows writes to +0x09 only from the per-frame HUD zero-writer (0x0801C9A0, every ~3 frames, always
0→0) plus the counter popup arm/decrement described next. **The ticket's cited asm00_2.s:27649 lines are
the draw gate's `cmp #6`/`cmp #5` on the countdown — a blink phase test, not a synchro store.**

## 2. What canon actually does on the counter hit (measured, A@40)

Cannon press at canon frame 40 → the shot lands at **frame 61 = press+21** (mettaur CurAction
0x0b→**0x04** flinch, same frame). On that frame:

| event | address | value | writer (from --watch-write) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| popup flag A | 0x02035288 (+0x08) | 0xff | strb at 0x0801E0E0 (lr 0x0801DAE6) |
| popup flag B | 0x0203528A (+0x0a) | 0xff | strb at 0x0801E0E2 (lr 0x0801DAE6) |
| popup countdown | 0x0203528B (+0x0b) | 0x3c→0 first-seen 0x3b at 61 | dec at 0x0801CA12 (lr 0x0801BEF8), 1/frame, gone ~122 |
| popup state byte | 0x0203528C (+0x0c) | 0→1 | same frame |
| emotion face slot | 0x02035290 (+0x10) halfword | 0x0000→**0x0003** | same frame, stays ≥60 frames |

The two 0xff flags + sounds + a 0x400-byte GFX queue are `sub_801E0DC` (asm00_2.s:30170-30192):
`strb 0xff [r5,#8]`, `strb 0xff [r5,#0xa]`, two `call_sub_3005EBA` sound-ish calls with ids **6** and
**0x1a**, then `sub_801BEC2(0x400)`. It is the **element bit 0x400 = index 0x0A** of the HUD dispatch
table `off_801DAF0` (dispatch_801DACC, asm00_2.s:29214-29271) — the counter popup is HUD element 0x400,
not an emotion-struct write. First/last popup frame measured: **61 .. ~122** (60-frame countdown).
Sound ids 6 and 0x1a fire on the hit frame (unverified audibly, ids from registers).

## 3. The face that appears is slot 3 (= enum 2, Mood==0xff), not slot 2

Resolved slot halfword 0x02035290 went 0x0003 on the hit frame and held. byte_801E6F4 resolves enum 2
(Mood==0xff) → slot 3. **No Mood byte flip was found** in the three banks watched (0x0203CCE0:0x10,
0x0203CB10:0x10, 0x02034A60:0x10 — all byte-stable across the hit), so canon either writes the slot
directly or reads a Mood source not yet located (next probe: --watch-write 0x02035290:2 to name the
writer; then the Mood load inside possiblyGetBattleEmotion_8015B64 asm00_2.s:15122-15174).
AIData.Unk_32 (0x020340B2) stayed 0 the whole time — the emotion_syn slot-2 route is a different state.

## 4. The in-window predicate is on the HIT FRAME, not the press frame

Three presses, same scenario (ALIVE):

| press | shot lands | mettaur CurAction after | popup? | slot→3? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A@40 | 61 (windup: swing began 32, shockwave at ~69) | 0x04 flinch | yes 61..~122 | yes |
| A@140 | 161 (windup of swing 2: 138..~175) | 0x04 flinch | yes 161.. | yes |
| A@60 | ~81 (post-shockwave, swing-frame 49) | 0x0b UNCHANGED (no flinch) | **no** | no |

So canon's rule: a hit landing while the enemy is mid-**windup** (attack executor entered, own shockwave
not yet spawned — the 0x40-frame pickaxe counter above 0x1b) = flinch + counter reaction; a hit landing
after the shockwave spawns = damage WITHOUT flinch (super-armor) and no counter. The no-ALIVE A@60 run
killed the mettaur (~40 HP), so damage IS still applied out of window; only the flinch/counter is
suppressed. The ticket's "A pressed within the swing window" is equivalent only via the fixed
press→hit delay of 21 frames on this route; the state the code tests must be the victim's attack timer
at the damage event (src/actor.rs already has the strike point: MettaurEntry strike_at 0x1b/0x40).

## 6. Why no port landed

The ticket's step 4 says exactly "write 1 to 0x0203528F (Full Synchro grant)". That write is (a) not what
canon does (§1) and (b) would corrupt the blink countdown on our side while producing none of the measured
behaviour. The measured mechanism (§2/§4) needs: element 0x400 popup arm (two 0xff flags, 60-frame
countdown, +0x0c=1, slot 3, sounds 6+0x1a) gated on "player damage lands in enemy windup", plus the
post-shockwave no-flinch armor arm. That is a different (and slightly wider) change than ticketed, and the
popup's PIXELS were not yet isolated (a first pos-vs-neg capture diff at frames 55+ shows mixed
flinch/shot deltas, total 34576/12 frames — needs same-press-region isolation before it can gate a
rendering port). Next worker: name the +0x10 writer, extract element 0x400's GFX source + screen region,
then port §2/§4 as above with the popup drawn through the popup path, and add `counter_hit_full` to
tools/states.py (mettaur base + A@40) with the A@60 negative.
