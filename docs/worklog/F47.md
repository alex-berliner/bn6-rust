## Result so far — the ticket's own register map FAILS its own test, on its own kept captures

Watches above kept and analysed (absolute paths, all under `/tmp/bn-f47/`):
`canon_scrollregs.bin`, `canon_scrollcnt.bin`, `rust_scrollregs.bin`, `rust_marker.bin`.

- rust marker origin = frame 8 (`rust_marker.bin`, magic 0x42415454 "BATT"), confirming the
  row's origin=8 / offset=113 -> captures 121..160 for k=0..39.
- COUNTERS CONFIRMED (`canon_scrollcnt.bin`, 16 B/frame, Counter0/Counter1 signed at +0/+4):
  frame 135 = -64216/-32108, falling 8/4 per frame, to -64528/-32264 at frame 174.
  `(c0>>4) & 511` gives 82/41 at k=0 -> 63/31 at k=39 — F46's confirmed ramp, reproduced
  byte-wise. This is the ground truth for canon's BG1 scroll write.
- THE WATCHED BLOCK 0x040000D0..0x040000DE DOES NOT HOLD SCROLL. Per-frame halfwords at
  D4/D6 (idx 2,3) on canon: 0x4211 / 0xD0FC / 0xFFFE / 0x4805 cycling, H and V halfwords
  IDENTICAL on every one of the 175 frames; rust: constant 0x30b8 on all 161 frames, H==V.
  Neither matches its own side's known scroll (canon's counters say 82/41..63/31; rust's
  picture scrolls all window). These are exactly the value families F46 saw and the verifier
  quoted (0xfffe, 0x4211, 0x30b8) — they appear at D4/D6 too, so they are not a window-
  register signature, they are readback noise for this whole region (H==V is the tell: no
  real per-register state). The watch path itself is a real read: mgba_capture.c:968-974 does
  `core->busRead8` per byte after every rendered frame (usage :85-90), so what it returns IS
  the emulated bus's answer for that address.
- REGISTER-MAP EVIDENCE, standard GBA layout: 0x04000010..0x0400001E are BG0HOFS..BG3VOFS
  (GBATEK video regs; WIN0H/WIN1H/WIN0V/WIN1V/WININ/WINOUT/MOSAIC are 0x04000040..0x0400004C),
  and 0x040000C8..0x040000DE is DMA2/DMA3. F46's verifier's correction — 'the scroll registers
  are 0x040000D0..0x040000DE, 0x04000010..0x0400001E are window/mosaic' — is INVERTED. Two
  independent confirmations already on disk: (1) F46's own smoke test read CHANGING values
  (05 48 -> 11 42 -> 11 42 -> fe ff = 0x4805, 0x4211, 0xFFFE) at 0x14/0x16 and its worklog
  called them live scroll; (2) this run's counters. The ticket's hard lesson inherited the
  inversion; the captures refute it. NOT yet quoted as a register table — next capture pair
  watches 0x04000000:0x60 (whole video IO block) on BOTH sides so the scroll halfwords are
  read from the block that both sides actually drive (canon BG1 = 0x14/0x16 per the asm strh
  targets oRenderInfo_BG1HOfs_10 -> BG1HOFS; rust BG0 = 0x10/0x12 per set_scroll_pos,
  src/backdrop.rs:317-320).
