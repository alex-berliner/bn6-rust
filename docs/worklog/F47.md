Edge convention (stated because it decides the headline; the previous F46 auditor's
"unreproducible" verdict was exactly this kind of ambiguity): the canon frame is translated
by (dx,dy) and compared ONLY on the overlap rectangle (240-|dx|)x(160-|dy|); the
translated-in/out edge bands are EXCLUDED from the comparison on both sides — shifted-in
pixels are NOT charged — and there is no wrap. The (2,1),(1,1),(2,1),(1,0) cycle as written
means canon's picture moves right/down by that many px to align with rust; in the sampling
convention canon'(x,y) = canon(x-dx,y-dy) the same cycle reads
(-2,-1),(-1,-1),(-2,-1),(-1,0). Recomputed from the kept dirs in the wording pass under both
conventions: overlap-only gives 39580 total and exact-0 on the 20 frames k=8-12,16-20,24-28,
32-36 (this run's numbers); the verifier's +-4 run that CHARGES shifted-in edge pixels gives
53937 and 0-of-34. So 39580 and 20-of-34 are convention-DEPENDENT numbers (this convention:
overlap-only, edges excluded), while the base 300946 and the period-4 cycle shape are not.
