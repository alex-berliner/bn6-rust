## The mechanism (named lines)

The band = the first ~22 scanlines of the reveal capture rendering the PRE-renumber hardware
BG0 = filler_bg = an always-empty background (battle.rs:2163-2168, blank screenblock sb17) =
transparent = black, BEFORE the renumber's register writes reach the PPU mid-display:

- agb's `GraphicsFrame::commit` (vendor/agb/agb/src/display/mod.rs:315-347) writes EVERYTHING —
  bg_ctrl registers, screenblock copies, blend, palette, gc — after `wait_for_vblank()`.
- agb's wait "returns immediately instead of blocking" when the vblank count has already moved
  (`last_waited_number < NUM_VBLANKS` check — quoted in src/main.rs:414-435's own doc for the
  first-commit case; the same check governs every commit). At the boundary the logic stretch
  (RESULT-window construction, marker stall 108 across captures 116-120) overruns its frame, so
  the renumber's register writes land MID-SCAN of capture 121 at scanline ~22: scanlines 0..21
  render the old BG0 (filler, black), 22+ render the new (backdrop art). The SEAM_PHASE_PAD doc
  (main.rs:309-322) already measured this spill class on the cursor row (k=37 seam, stale-vs-new).
- Canon's equivalent discipline: its copies are vblank-QUEUED (sub_8001C94 ->
  QueueEightWordAlignedGFXTransfer, reference/bn6f/asm/asm00_0.s:3752), so its same-transition
  writes land between captures — T25's measured model ("canon's counter-to-register is
  same-capture", harness FIELD_ZERO note) vs ours one-capture-late/mid-scan.

Which canon-side reading governs a CELL PAINT phase: NEITHER of the ticket's two candidates
alone. T133's loader reading (LoadBGAnimData tiles-then-map, asm03_0.s:21223-21266 through
sub_8080DA0, asm21.s:25-37) governs ART CONTENT — and our art content is already right (watch:
resident since cap 115). T137's arming reading (sub_8007978 asm00_1.s:9594-9615 ->
sub_80079A8 :9616-9648 writing oBattleState_Unk_0d) governs RESULT arming, one capture of the
verdict store, not a paint phase. The band's phase is owned by the COMMIT-VS-SCAN timing of the
renumber's register writes (the wait discipline above), which lives in vendor/agb's commit +
src/main.rs's frame loop — NOT in src/backdrop.rs.

CAVEAT (unresolved, do not trust): the HOFS/VOFS watch bytes read a frozen 184/184 at captures
121-124 and pre-boundary pairs (498/242, 256/0, 82/82) that match NEITHER the port's seeded
scroll formula (x~339, y~41 at battle 108-110) nor zero — BGxHOFS/VOFS are WRITE-ONLY registers
(mgba itself warns "Read from write-only I/O register: 018/01A" during the watch), so these
readbacks do not reflect the rendered state; the row's k>=1 zeros (art byte-equal canon while
the register reads frozen) prove the readback is the artifact. Discard that thread; the BGxCNT
reads are readable registers and are trusted.

## Step 3 verdict: bounded NEGATIVE — no src/backdrop.rs change can reach it; the levers are named and forbidden

move this band;
(c) an alignment/pairing change — FORBIDDEN (the three field rows are frozen).

STOP and report which one it needs"): it needs (a) or (b) — most precisely, making the
RESULT-window construction NOT overrun its frame (split the shown-background construction
across frames, or force the boundary commit's wait to actually block), which is battle.rs/main.rs
work for the user to assign. T133's two rejected attempts stay rejected: the value-major scan
