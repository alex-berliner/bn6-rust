### Step 4 — Findings (NOT FIXED, budget exhausted)

Per AGENT_GUIDE's rule: "If the number did not move as the ticket predicted,
say so and stop; do not widen the change to make it move." I am stopping
here. Three investigation paths left for the next worker:

1. Try damage_apply as a non-`pub` `#[inline(always)]` directly inlined at
   the call sites (no separate function, no .rodata table). The T146 attempt
   (a) row was 1/1/170 with that shape; my `pub` + separate .rodata shape is
   not it.
2. Or skip damage_apply entirely and inline `(chip.power as u32).saturating_mul(1)`
   = `chip.power` at the sword/airshot sites -- the multiplier is 1 for every
   existing row's enemy (Mettaur elem=0 weak=0; the fixture-side override is
   the M3 follow-up). This is a no-op port that sets up the harness row but
   does not actually exercise the multiplier.
3. Or commit the wiring as-is and accept the cursor regression as known +
   tiny (10 px on 170 frames = 0.005% pixel change). The ticket's acceptance
   says "cursor byte-identical" -- 10 px ≠ byte-identical.

The element_hit harness row was NOT added because:
- It needs the fixture-side enemy_element + enemy_secondary_weakness override
  to land first (T146 BLOCKED worklog step 5 + 7 also flagged this).
- Adding the row without that override produces a row that does not exercise
  the multiplier (multiplier=1 for every existing fixture enemy).

The override itself needs new bytes after +63, extending the descriptor past
its current 64-byte boundary. The cleanest place is +64 (panel data already
extends to +62, +63 is overlay-claimed by emotion/enemy_action; +64/+65 are
free). Two u8 fields × 3 slots = 6 bytes total fits.
