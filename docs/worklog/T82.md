## What to try next

A real M6 advancement needs **at least one** of the four prereqs
to land. Suggested ticket split:

1. **`T-NAVI-KIND` (or similar) — `Style::Navi` arm + `KIND_NAVI`
   family**: add a `Style::Navi` variant (with a `NaviKind` sub-
   enum or a `u8 ai_index: 5` field), a new `KIND_NAVI: u8 = 2`
   const, and a wire-up in `src/battle.rs`'s `enemy_think` that
   delegates to a new `src/navi.rs` `For810A9EC` (AIIndex 0x03)
   and `For810A550` (AIIndex 0x02) entry structs in parallel.
   This is the prereq that closes the most M6 paths at once.

2. **`T-NAVI-SETTINGS` — find the BattleSettings record carrying
   AIIndex 0x10D..0x112**: walk `EnemySetupArr` for entries whose
   first slot's enemy_idx is in the navi 0x10D..0x1C3 range and
   whose `byte_80B5347` flag pattern matches a navi-only record;
   identify the `iCurrFrame`-style lever that picks it (likely a
   different lever than T9c's virus-route one). This is the
   "no state to bake" blocker; without it, even with the engine
   support, no harness row can be built.

3. **`T-BANNER-UNSTICK` — close T9d's sequencer stick on the
   navi-only path**: this is the deepest blocker and most likely
   a separate piece of work (T9d's Start@160 follow-up is a
   start, but the navi-only encounter needs its own menu-close
   measurement). Recommend as a single dedicated ticket — M6
   cannot advance until at least the first two are landed AND
   this one is partially understood (a navi row can be measured
   on the partial-stick if the sequencer advances past 0x04 even
   on the navi route).

If only #1 lands, M6 advances from 0/25 to **2/25** (the AIIndex
0x02 and 0x03 per-type entries are both ported in `src/navi.rs`,
but no harness row can be added). If #1 and #2 land, M6 can
advance to **1/25 verified** (one harness row, AIIndex 0x02
first, per T79's recipe shape). If all three land, M6 is fully
unblocked.
