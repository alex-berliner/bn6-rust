## Step 4 — what the watch actually says about the attack-event claim

The T9j comment in tools/harness.py:2478 ("canon_ref=80 -- the gunner's first
attack-event frame -- CurAction transitioning to 0x0A, when sub_8113162's
row-check fires -- is 80") is **not supported** by the watch on canon's
battlestart_gunner.state:

- `0x0203ab69` (CurAction) reads 0x00 from frame 0 to frame 150, then 0x01 from
  frame 151. It never reaches 0x0A within 300 captured frames.
- `0x02034320` (AIAttackVars_Unk_00, the byte sub_8112D9C dispatches on at
  asm32.s:9737) reads 0x00 throughout the capture.
- `0x0203CA70` (banner sequencer dword, per T9d) stays 0 — the battle never goes
  live without input.

T9d's "the battle never goes live" is the truth. The first observable Gunner
state-byte event in the harness's k=6..75 band is `CurPhase 0x00 -> 0x04` at
canon frame **118** (k=38 in the old window) -- an internal phase change inside
`RunSpawnAnimationMaybe_8016380` during CurAction 0x00, not a gunner-attack event.

The harness-side alignment at canon_ref=80 picks up SOMETHING (a 2.1M total vs
0/0/130 PASS ideal), but the 1.4M BG3 plateau is the chip-window timing gap
(rust's `gauge=0x4000` opens the chip window at k=6+; canon's gauge-full pause
opens it at k=77). That gap is **71 frames**, larger than the locked search
band `range(0, 40)` -- no offset in 0..40 can bridge it. Until a src/-side seed
work lifts the rust-side gauge from `0x00` to `0x4000` at the same k canon
fires its, the residue is inherent.

## Decision

**`GUNNER_ROW.align.canon_ref` stays at 80.** The ticket's predicted mechanism
(settle window at sub_8112D9C, k=6..75 band, gate ForGunner_8113078) does not
apply in this state -- the gunner does not reach the attack-event settle window
in canon's battlestart_gunner.state because the battle never goes live (T9d's
verified finding). The only "event" in the k=6..75 band is an internal spawn
phase change (CurPhase 0x00->0x04 at frame 118) that the harness's existing
alignment already finds a local minimum for.

The expected <1,500,000 needs the **rust-side gauge seed** to actually take
effect (F40a-deferred src/ work), not a descriptor-only align move. The 71-frame
BG3 chip-window plateau gap is wider than the locked search band.

A NEGATIVE: the "CurAction transitioning to 0x0A at f80" claim in the existing
tools/harness.py:2478 comment is contradicted by canon's own memory at that
frame (CurAction = 0x00; AIAttackVars_Unk_00 = 0x00; banner sequencer = 0).
This is a docstring defect that the next pass on the row should fix, but it
does not block landing -- it explains why the row's pixel output is what it is.

## Mechanism (one line)

Canon's battlestart_gunner.state never advances the gunner past CurAction 0x01
in the captured window (T9d's "battle never goes live"); the 71-frame BG3
chip-window plateau gap (rust opens at k=6+, canon at k=77) is wider than the
locked search band `range(0, 40)`, so canon_ref alone cannot close it.
