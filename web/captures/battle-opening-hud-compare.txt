HISTORY (2026-09-08): made by a tool that no longer exists, under that day's method (a boxed region or a demo-feature build), so any 'pixels a frame' figure below is not today's full-screen standard. Kept as a record of that step.

The HUD and the backdrop through a battle's opening, canon above and this build below: 44 consecutive frames, 0 pixels, every frame compared rather than sampled. The backdrop is not just a scrolling picture -- it also ANIMATES, on the same scripted engine the overworld uses, ten four-frame steps then nineteen eight-frame ones over a 192-frame loop, and this build had been running a uniform 56-frame loop instead. The scroll matters to the pixel too: canon writes a logical shift of a counter that falls, which rounds the opposite way from a plain divide and puts the whole layer one pixel out on odd frames. The check that first said this matched was sampling every fourth frame, and every fourth frame was exactly the set that already agreed.
