Everything the script drives -- the cursor, the card, the name -- was exact for a long time while the highlight bracket's blink stayed one frame out, and that was written off as unfixable: the blink counts from the window's opening, the canon side is a save state already open, so the two clocks were said to have no shared origin. Reading the routine instead of guessing at it settled it.
