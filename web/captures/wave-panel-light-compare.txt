The shockwave's panel light, canon (sterile) above and this build below, backgrounds only so the wave's own sprite is hidden and only the yellow highlight it leaves on each panel shows: 90 frames, 0 pixels. This was the last non-zero check in the suite. The hop itself was never the hard part -- what took three attempts was the ends of a segment's life. A hop spawns a NEW segment and abandons the old one where it is, and our build was destroying the abandoned one a frame before it could show its final animation frame, and lighting the newly spawned one a frame late. The real ROM has the same asymmetry in its own code: the handler that runs on a segment's first tick builds its sprite and collision data and never highlights, and highlighting only begins on the tick after.
