The chip window closing. Before F3 (middle), a faded copy of the window stayed on the field after the slide-out -- our code waited for a scroll position the slide never reaches, so the window's tiles were never cleared. F3 clears the columns as the slide passes them, as canon does (bottom): the window's own layer now matches on all ten slide frames. The row's total did not move at this commit because it measures the whole screen during and after the close; the post-close band was fixed later in F18.
